🧘 Daily Routine, Posture & Progression Rules (Reconciled & Evidence-Based)

Caloric Target & Deficit: Maintenance ~2,250–2,350 kcal. Lifting Days: ~1,900 kcal (-400 deficit). Rest Days: ~1,700 kcal (-400/-350 deficit). Average daily deficit ~385 kcal/day producing predictable 0.3–0.4 kg/week fat loss while maintaining full muscle mass. Target 8,000–10,000 steps daily (NEAT).
Protein Target & Whole-Food Nutrition (Zero Whey): 145–160g/day (calibrated at 1.75–1.9g/kg). Daily Anchor Dinner: 500g raw chicken breast (~113g protein, ~575 kcal) + 200g dahi (~8g protein) + 2 scoops BeastLife Creatine (6g creatine). Office Days: Office lunch (2 rotis, dal, sabji, chawal, sides ~20g) + 4 PM snack (~8g) + dinner = ~150g protein (~1,850 kcal). WFH Days: Midday meal (60–75g air-fried soya chunks ~35g protein OR 4 boiled eggs ~25g protein) + dinner = ~146–156g protein (~1,250–1,400 kcal).
Morning Posture & TVA: Fasted Stomach Vacuum (3 sets × 15–20s) to train deep transverse abdominis motor control + Daily Kneeling Hip-Flexor Stretch (2 × 45s/side) to release tight psoas from desk sitting.
Overload & Rest Protocols: 1–2 RIR on heavy compounds (Rest 2–3m); 0–1 RIR on isolations (Rest 60–75s). Add reps before jumping dumbbell increments. Deload every 6–8 weeks by halving volume for 1 week.

Wednesday: Active Recovery & Mobility
Cardio & Mobility

Desk Worker Total Posture Reset (30 Mins)

• 20–25 Mins Incline Walk: Treadmill speed 4.0–4.8 km/h at 6–8% incline (Low-Intensity Steady State cardio for active recovery and NEAT).
