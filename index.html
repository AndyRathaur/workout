Wednesday: Active Recovery & Mobility
Cardio & Mobility

Desk Worker Total Posture Reset (30 Mins)

• 20–25 Mins Incline Walk: Treadmill speed 4.5–5.0 km/h at 8–10% incline (Zone 2 fat burning, zero knee impact).
• Dead Hang from Bar: 2 sets × 30–45s (decompresses spine, stretches tight pec minor & lats, relieves desk hunch).
• Couch Stretch: 45 seconds per leg (opens tight hip flexors shortened by prolonged sitting to fix APT).
• Cat-Cow & Thoracic Rotations: 2 sets × 10 reps (restores upper-back mobility to reverse rounded shoulders).
• Bird-Dogs: 2 sets × 10 reps per side (reinforces spinal stabilization and glute firing).
