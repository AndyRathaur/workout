Exercise Setup:

🧘 Everyday Morning Ritual: The Stomach Vacuum

When: Every morning directly after washroom/potty on an empty stomach.
How: Exhale all air out, suck belly button into spine, hold tight for 15–20s. Do 3 sets.
Why: Tightens internal core belt to keep stomach flat all day.

Monday: Upper Body A
Chest, Width & Triceps
Exercise 1
Dumbbell Setup (Recommended) Swipe for Barbell Rod 👉
Incline Dumbbell Bench Press
3 Sets × 8–10 Reps
Target: Upper Pectorals (Square Masculine Chest Shelf)
⚡ Dumbbell Advantage: Deeper stretch below chest level + safe to drop weights when training alone.
Bench at 30°–45°. Retract shoulder blades. Lower over 2s to collarbones, press up in a gentle arc. Rest 90s.
Barbell Rod Setup 👈 Swipe for Dumbbells

3 Sets × 8–10 Reps
Target: Upper Pectorals & Raw Pressing Power
⚡ Barbell Advantage: Easier micro-loading with plates. Stop 1 rep shy of failure for safety without a spotter.
Medium grip outside shoulders. Lower rod under control to collarbone level, drive up explosively. Rest 90s.

Wednesday: Active Recovery & Mobility
Cardio & Mobility

Desk Worker Mobility Routine (30 Mins)

• 20–25 Mins Incline Walk: Treadmill speed 4.5–5.0 km/h at 8–10% incline (Zone 2 fat burning, zero knee impact).
